Welcome to Retro1996 Browser! You are about to experience the internet as it existed in 1996 -- before portals, before broadband, when the web was young, simple, and full of wonder.

This browser recreates the authentic 1996 web browsing experience with period-accurate rendering of HTML 3.2, CSS1, and JavaScript.

Whether you are exploring web history, testing legacy websites, or simply curious about how the internet looked in the 1990s, Retro1996 offers a window into the past.
